What you can do

From first report to analyst investigation, the platform keeps evidence separate from conclusions.

Report threats

Describe suspicious activity, attach indicators like URLs, IPs or hashes, and submit in seconds.

Cross-reference intelligence

Submissions are checked against known threat indicators to surface potential matches as evidence.

Analyst investigation

Authorized analysts review reports, record assessments and maintain a full audit trail.

How reporting works

A transparent four-step process with no automatic promotion of complaints.

01

Submit

Provide details about the suspicious activity and any indicators.

02

Match

The system checks indicators against known threats as evidence only.

03

Investigate

An analyst reviews the report and records a reasoned assessment.

04

Resolve

The report is marked confirmed, unsubstantiated, or closed.
