How the platform works

A clear separation between reported complaints and verified threat intelligence.

Reporting

Anyone can submit a report about suspicious activity.

  • Describe what happened and the suspected threat category.
  • Provide indicators such as URLs, IP addresses, domains or file hashes.
  • Add optional source context (email subject, website, message).

Matching (evidence, not proof)

Submitted indicators are compared against the threat intelligence database.

  • Matches are shown as potential leads to the reporter and analysts.
  • A match does not automatically confirm a threat or change report status.
  • Reports and verified threats remain separate records.

Investigation

Authorized analysts review reports in a protected workspace.

  • Analysts record an assessment: Pending Review, Under Investigation, Confirmed Threat, Unsubstantiated, or Closed.
  • Investigation notes and an audit trail are maintained for transparency.
  • No default analyst account is shipped with the application; accounts are created via a documented CLI command.

Privacy and data

Sample data uses documentation ranges (example.com, RFC 5737 IPs) and is labelled illustrative.

This platform is an educational project. Do not submit real personal data you are not authorized to share.
